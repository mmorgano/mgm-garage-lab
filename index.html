02 / Lab method

Projects are not all products.

Some become tools. Some stay experiments. The lab is where an engineering idea earns its shape through building, testing and documenting the tradeoffs.

Build

Make the idea concrete enough that its limits become visible.

Test

Use real workflows and honest constraints, not polished fiction.

Learn

Keep decisions and findings readable for the next iteration.

Share

Publish what is clean, independent and useful to others.
